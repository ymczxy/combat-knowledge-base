# v1.6.1 Batch 08 - M2 Browning vertical slice

## Scope

This batch adds a source-checked M2 Browning heavy machine gun entity and a canonical 12.7 x 99 mm cartridge entity, linked through one independent `uses_ammunition` assertion.

## Evidence boundary

- The U.S. Army equipment portfolio supports the M2 designation, .50 caliber / 12.7 mm designation, recoil operation, belt feed, air cooling, mounting context and stated cyclic-rate range.
- The U.S. Army M2A1 article supports the M2 family and official service context.
- NATO AEP-97 identifies 12.7 mm x 99 ammunition and links under STANAG / AOP 4383; this is used as the standardization and compatibility evidence boundary.
- Compatibility is not inferred from calibre alone; it is represented by the explicit official weapon description and NATO standard references.

## Safety and gameplay boundary

The batch contains no loading recipes, propellant charges, primer specifications, manufacturing tolerances, manufacturing guidance, attack procedures, target-selection advice or gameplay balance values.
