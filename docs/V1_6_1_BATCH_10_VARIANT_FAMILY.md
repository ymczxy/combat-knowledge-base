# v1.6.1 Batch 10 - Variant / Family relation closure

## Scope

This batch adds a source-checked Thompson submachine gun family entity and an independent `variant_of` assertion from the existing Thompson M1A1 model.

## Evidence boundary

- Royal Armouries supports the Thompson family identity and submachine-gun classification.
- National Army Museum supports the wartime Thompson family context and the A1-pattern context used by the existing M1A1 record.
- The relation means “named model is a variant of the named family”; it does not mean direct development, influence, same calibre or interchangeable components.
- Family-level claims remain qualified and are not used to fill model-level measurements.

## Safety boundary

Only public identification, family boundaries and provenance are represented. No operational instructions, manufacturing guidance, loading data, attack planning or gameplay balance values are included.
