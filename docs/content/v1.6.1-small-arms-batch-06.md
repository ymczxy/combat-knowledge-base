# v1.6.1 Small Arms Batch 06

## Scope

This batch adds a source-checked Thompson M1A1 and `.45 ACP / 45 Auto` compatibility chain.

## Evidence boundary

- Royal Armouries supports the M1 Thompson identification, submachine-gun category and `.45 calibre` wording.
- National Army Museum supports the wartime Thompson family context and the use of 20- or 30-round box magazines on simplified A1-pattern wartime examples.
- C.I.P. supports `45 Auto` as the standards-body designation in its pistol and revolver cartridge table.
- Family-level statements are explicitly qualified and are not silently promoted into unqualified object-specific measurements.

## Safety boundary

The ammunition entity stores identification and compatibility metadata only. It excludes loading recipes, propellant charges, primer specifications, manufacturing tolerances, attack instructions and gameplay balance values.

## Relationship

`Thompson M1A1 -> uses_ammunition -> .45 ACP / 45 Auto`
