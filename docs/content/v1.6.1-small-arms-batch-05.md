# v1.6.1 Small Arms Batch 05

This batch adds the PPSh-41 and the 7.62×25 mm Tokarev cartridge as a source-checked vertical slice.

## Evidence boundary

- National Army Museum supports the PPSh-41 category, broad production/service context and 71-round drum statement.
- Imperial War Museums object FIR 6119 supports a 1942 production date, 84.5 cm overall length, 27 cm barrel length and 7.62×25 calibre for the catalogued object.
- Object-specific dimensions remain qualified and are not generalized to every production variation.

## Safety boundary

The records contain identification, public dimensions, historical context and compatibility metadata only. They exclude loading recipes, propellant charges, primer specifications, manufacturing tolerances, attack instructions and gameplay balance values.
