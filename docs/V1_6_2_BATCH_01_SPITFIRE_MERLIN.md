# v1.6.2 Batch 01 — Spitfire / Merlin vertical slice

This batch starts the aviation expansion with a WWII propeller-aircraft and engine chain:

- `ckb:platform:air:spitfire_mk_i`
- `ckb:component:engine:rolls_royce_merlin_ii`
- `uses_engine` relation stored independently in `data/relationships/v1_6_2_aviation_batch_01.json`

The aircraft record keeps its Merlin II identification tied to the RAF Museum K9942 collection record. The IWM Merlin history provides family-level context and a bounded power range; those claims remain qualified and are not treated as the exact output of every Merlin II installation.

The batch is deliberately limited to public identification and high-level technical facts. It excludes maintenance, construction, flight-operation and weapon-employment instructions.
